Access Admin Console

  • Scan QR code above or open the Admin app icon on your home screen
  • Enter your Admin PIN on the keypad
  • PIN stays active for the session — no re-entry needed until you close the app
Keep your PIN confidential. Do not share with drivers.
2

Swap Requests Tab

  • View all duty swap forms submitted by drivers
  • Filter by status: All · Awaiting Driver B · Awaiting Supervisor · Completed
  • Search by driver name
  • Tap any card to open the full swap form (Screen 4)
3

Mailing List Tab

  • Enter the separate Mailing List PIN to unlock
  • Manage who receives swap notification emails
  • Toggle Active to include/exclude from emails
  • Toggle Authority to Sign to appear as selectable supervisor on approval screen
  • Add or remove supervisors at any time
Authority to Sign must be enabled for supervisor to appear in the approval dropdown.

Swap Status Reference

Status Meaning
Awaiting Driver B Driver A submitted — waiting for Driver B to sign
Awaiting Supervisor Both drivers signed — waiting for supervisor approval
Completed Supervisor signed — swap is fully approved
